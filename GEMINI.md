# Fonno

Fonno keeps the books of a Norwegian company and gets its filings ready for Skatteetaten. The owner approves and files.

- Start with `fonno_overview`. It says which company you are connected to and what is due.
- Every tool that changes the books says so, and Gemini asks before calling it. Show the owner what will change first.
- Amounts are in NOK, dates are `YYYY-MM-DD`, and a posting must balance.
- A delivery to Skatteetaten is the owner's decision. Prepare it, show it, and deliver only when they say so.
